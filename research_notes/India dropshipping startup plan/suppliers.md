# Dropshipping Suppliers and Platforms for Indian Shopify Stores (as of October 2026)

> Method note for the report writer: direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) were blocked by the session's network policy, so every finding below comes from web-search result summaries and snippets. I could not open Shopify App Store pages, supplier pricing pages, Trustpilot or Reddit directly. Many of the sources are blogs run by competing vendors (Qikink, Blinkstore, Eprolo, Spocket, GridRay, and HeadlessBiz, which promotes Snazzyway), so they have commercial bias. I flag where it matters. No Reddit threads or YouTube reviews surfaced in any search, so reputation evidence comes from Trustpilot, the Shopify App Store, the Apple App Store, ConsumerComplaints.in, MouthShut and Justdial.

## Q1. Supplier and platform comparison: fees, Shopify integration, range, margins, shipping/COD/RTO, reputation, and who is active and trustworthy in 2026

### Takeaway
The Indian dropshipping market changed a lot in 2026. **Roposo Clout, once the default Indian Shopify dropship app, shut its marketplace on 1 April 2026.** GlowRoad's status under Amazon is unclear, and its Shopify app may have been delisted. The main catalog suppliers still running are **DeoDap** (Rajkot; home, kitchen and gadgets; about ₹5,000–6,000 a year plus GST) and **Baapstore** (₹13,000–31,000+ a year, with frequent price changes and many complaints), plus a handful of small Shopify apps with weak ratings (Wukusy, Dropship India, Dropdash, Dropshipera). For print-on-demand, **Qikink** has the best COD and RTO terms. Meesho works as a sourcing channel for resellers but is not a Shopify integration. None of these has a clean reputation. Every one shows recurring complaints about delays, incomplete orders or refunds, so test orders are essential.

- Because every catalog supplier has visible complaints about delays, partial orders and refunds, "trustworthy" is relative. DeoDap has the most reviews and the lowest entry fee, so it is the lowest-risk catalog supplier to test with. Baapstore's high upfront fees combined with its refund complaints make it the riskiest.

- For GD Home Fabric's niche (curtains, pillows, bedding), no Indian dropship app is a strong source of quality home textiles. Direct sourcing (Q3) is the better fit, with DeoDap or Dropship India as an add-on source for kitchen and home gadgets.

## Q2. Is importing from China (AliExpress/CJ) to Indian customers viable in 2026?

### Takeaway
Mostly no, for a COD-driven Indian store. AliExpress has been blocked in India since 2020 and blogs say checkout and shipping to India are disabled. CJ and Eprolo ship from China (CJ's India warehouse is unconfirmed) with 7–40 day transit. India has no de minimis exemption for commercial parcels, so duty plus IGST applies. China-sourced lines don't support COD, which most Indian shoppers expect. Bulk importing for your own inventory is a separate, workable route; per-order dropshipping from China is not.

### Cited Findings
- India banned AliExpress among Chinese apps in 2020 under Section 69A of the IT Act. One guide says the site can be browsed but **checkout and shipping to India remain disabled** (low-authority blogs; another claims workarounds via forwarders) — [AliBestFinds](https://alibestfinds.com/aliexpress-india-shopping-guide/); [Alibaba Electronics Q&A](https://electronics.alibaba.com/question/aliexpress-in-india-shipping,-safety-workarounds-(2025))
- Shein, banned in 2020, returned to India in early 2025 under an arrangement in which India's largest retailer runs the platform — [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-02-15/shein-returns-to-india-after-five-year-ban-garment-workers/104923638); [SupplyChainBrain](https://www.supplychainbrain.com/articles/41144-shein-relaunches-in-india-as-five-year-ban-ends)
- **No de minimis for commercial goods.** India has no general low-value exemption; the only narrow carve-out is for genuine gifts up to about ₹5,000, and customs often reclassify parcels labelled as gifts — [iWishBag de minimis table](https://www.iwishbag.com/de-minimis); [Zonos India guide](https://zonos.com/docs/guides/country-guides/india)
- **Duty stack:** Basic Customs Duty of 0–40%, a 10% Social Welfare Surcharge on the BCD, and IGST, for roughly 42% on top of item price in one example — [HereWeShip 2026 guide](https://hereweship.com/en/shipping-to-india-from-us-2026-ecommerce-guide/); [DHL India duty guide](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-my/logistics-advice/import-export-advice/guide-to-import-duty-and-taxes-in-india). One unverified source claims BCD on personal courier imports fell from 20% to 10% in 2026 — [CouriersToIndia](https://courierstoindia.com/india-international-courier-rules-2026/)
- **Transit:** CJ Packet to India takes 10–25 days Registered, 20–40 days Ordinary and 7–15 days Express, with Diwali and customs delays — [CourierTrackingz](https://couriertrackingz.com/cj-packet-tracking/). Special lines from China to India take 7–15 days, and Indian sellers may face import duties and GST issues — [Zik Analytics](https://www.zikanalytics.com/blog/dropshipping-suppliers-india/). Domestic Indian suppliers take 3–9 days versus 12–25 days China-sourced — [Digital With Ankur](https://digitalwithankur.com/dropshipping-suppliers-in-india/)
- CJ itself says India shipping is slower, customs tighter, and "many dropshippers avoid marketing to India" — [CJ blog](https://cjdropshipping.com/blogs/logistics-&-supply-chain/Dropshipping-Shipping-Strategies-and-Methods). Eprolo ships only from China and US warehouses and only commits to refund India orders after 60 days — [Eprolo shipping](https://eprolo.com/shipping-methods)
- A Quora answer says customs officers may charge unpredictable duties, Indian shoppers can find cheap Chinese goods locally, and customers expect COD — [Quora](https://www.quora.com/Is-CJ-dropshipping-a-good-option-for-my-drop-shipping-business-in-India)
- **GST:** If a China supplier ships directly to your customer, you are still the seller and must charge GST on the supply. Sources conflict on the registration threshold (₹40 lakh vs ₹20 lakh) — [Swipe GST for dropshipping](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/gst-for-dropshipping); [Qikink legal guide](https://qikink.com/blog/what-are-dropshipping-legal-requirements-in-india/)

### Inferences
- Duties of roughly 40% or more, 2–5 week transit, no COD and customs uncertainty together make per-order China dropshipping uncompetitive against domestic suppliers and Meesho's prices. The viable China route is bulk import (with an IEC, paying duty once) into Indian stock, or a 3PL.
- Buyers who receive an unexpected duty demand on delivery are likely to refuse the parcel, adding RTO cost (inferred from the duty and COD findings).

### Gaps
- No official CBIC notification was found confirming 2026 courier duty rates. The sources list IGST slabs of 5/12/18/28%, but India's GST rate restructuring (reported for September 2025) may have changed import IGST slabs. This was not verified in this session.
- The sources conflict on the exact date of the AliExpress ban (one says 2 September 2020 with 117 other apps; I did not verify this against a MeitY order).
- No current confirmation that CJ's India warehouse still exists (only a 2020 CJ blog). Temu's India status was not found.

## Q3. Moving to private label and direct sourcing: clusters, MOQs, sample costs, and manufacturer dropshipping

### Takeaway
Indian home-textile clusters offer workable small MOQs for a starting brand. Typical listed MOQs: Jaipur block-print bedsheets 5–50 pieces, Panipat curtains 50–500 pieces, Panipat cushion covers 100–500, Karur bedsheets 30–100, Moradabad metal decor 10–100. Surat and Delhi Sadar Bazaar suit catalogue or one-catalogue buying. Samples are usually charged but often credited against the first bulk order. Directory listings rarely mention blind shipping, so a supplier-dropship arrangement has to be negotiated directly.

## Q4. Vetting steps for a new dropshipper

### Takeaway
Before scaling ads: verify the supplier's GSTIN and company age, place 2–5 sample orders to different addresses (including one COD order to a Tier-2 PIN code), and measure order-to-tracking time, packaging, accuracy and branding leaks. Get the dispatch SLA, the RTO/return policy and the COD fees in writing. Then keep monitoring defect, stockout and dispatch metrics, with a backup supplier ready.

### Cited Findings
- On a test order, measure order confirmation speed, packaging quality, product accuracy, dispatch time, tracking visibility, delivery time and support responsiveness. Poor packaging, wrong labelling or visible damage are serious warning signs — [LooperBuy](https://looperbuy.com/blog/how-to-test-dropshipping-suppliers-before-scaling-ads.html)
- Measure processing time from order to a real, scanning tracking number against the promised window, and check for supplier branding, invoices or inserts in the parcel — [ecom-store.pro checklist](https://shopify.ecom-store.pro/blog/dropshipping-supplier-vetting-checklist/)
- Verify the GSTIN on the official GST portal and the company's registration age on the MCA portal; one guide treats suppliers under 3 years old as a risk. Order samples to different addresses and from different batches — [Nventory](https://nventory.io/blog/drop-shipping-suppliers-in-india)
- Get the average dispatch time and the defect return or replacement policy in writing — [HeadlessBiz checklist](https://www.headlessbiz.com/post/shopify-dropshipping-checklist-what-to-set-up-before-you-launch)
- Order 2–5 samples, and confirm single-unit shipping and carrier coverage, including Northeast and remote PIN codes — [Spocket IndiaMART guide](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/indiamart-dropshipping)
- Test COD support if you plan to sell into Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities — [Digital With Ankur](https://digitalwithankur.com/dropshipping-suppliers-in-india/). "Always order a sample and verify COD + return policy before committing to any supplier" — [CIIM 2026 guide](https://www.ciim.in/dropshipping-in-india-is-roposo-closing-best-alternatives-you-must-know-2026-guide/)
- Keep tracking dispatch time, defect rate, response time and stockouts, and switch to a backup supplier if they slip — [ecom-store.pro](https://shopify.ecom-store.pro/blog/dropshipping-supplier-vetting-checklist/)
- On IndiaMART, check for TrustSEAL, Verified Exporter, IEC and GST, use "Know Your Seller" to match bank details, and pay only to the verified account — [IndiaMART Trust & Assurance](https://export.indiamart.com/trustworthiness/); [IndiaMART Payment Protection](https://buyer.indiamart.com/payment-protection)
- Specific red flags from the complaint record: paid membership followed by mostly out-of-stock items (Baapstore, 2019) — [ComplaintLists](https://www.complaintlists.com/baapstore-com-refund-not-received/); partial shipments after upfront payment (DeoDap) — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/deodap-dropshipping-wholesale/id1661765661?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone); undisclosed per-order COD fees (Wukusy, ₹25) — [Wukusy reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/wukusy-indian-dropshipping/reviews); and "no free trial, no samples" (Baapstore) — [HeadlessBiz](https://www.headlessbiz.com/baapstore-reviews)
- Confirm addresses over WhatsApp and use address-verification tools to cut COD RTO — [Qikink: Meesho Dropshipping](https://qikink.com/blog/meesho-dropshipping/)

### Inferences
- Given the platform's 2026 shutdown (Roposo) and fee volatility (Baapstore), vetting should also cover the platform itself. Avoid paying long annual or lifetime memberships upfront; prefer monthly or low-fee tiers (DeoDap at about ₹5,900) until fulfilment quality is proven. Keep exportable copies of invoices, since Roposo sellers lost the ability to regenerate them.
- Ask for the RTO charge per parcel, who pays return shipping, how long undelivered stock is held (Qikink's 100-day hold is a useful benchmark), and payout timing, all in writing.

### Gaps
- No Indian-specific data on typical supplier dispatch SLAs beyond vendor claims (DeoDap's "5–7 days" promise against reviews of 15–20 days).
- No source gave a recommended number of test COD orders or an RTO threshold for dropping a supplier.
