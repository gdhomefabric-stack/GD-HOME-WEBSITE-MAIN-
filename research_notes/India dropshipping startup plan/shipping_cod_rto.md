# Shipping, COD and RTO Economics for an India-only Dropshipping Store (2026, INR)

> Research method note (13 Oct 2026): direct page fetches to shiprocket.in, nimbuspost.com, ithinklogistics.com, base.com etc. were blocked in this environment (DNS failure / egress proxy 403). Every figure below comes from web-search results that quote or summarise those pages, so **no official rate card was read in full**. Treat per-500g rates as indicative and confirm them in each aggregator's rate calculator after signing up (free). Most "rate" sources are third-party calculators or vendor blogs, and they often disagree. Those disagreements are flagged inline.

## 1. Shipping aggregators: plans, zone rates, weight rules, COD fees, RTO charges, remittance

### Takeaway
For a new low-volume store, the free tiers (Shiprocket Lite at ₹0/month, NimbusPost's entry plan, Shipway, iThink) are enough. Expect roughly ₹20–26 per 500g in the cheapest zone/courier, ₹38–57 per 500g for Zone D "rest of India" and ₹52–110 per 500g for NE/J&K, all before 18% GST. Add a COD fee of about ₹27–45 or 1.5–2.5% of order value, whichever is higher. RTO freight runs at roughly 80–100% of forward freight. Chargeable weight is the higher of actual weight and L×B×H(cm)/5000. That divisor matters most for bulky-but-light home furnishings.

### Cited Findings

**Bulky home-furnishing implication (pillows, cushions, curtains)**
- A pillow in a 60×40×30 cm box weighing 3 kg bills at 14.4 kg volumetric. Vacuum-compressed into 40×30×15 cm, it bills at 3.6 kg. The vendor claims up to 75% savings. — [ShipPrime volumetric guide](https://shipprime.live/resources/blogs/volumetric-weight-vs-actual-weight)
- Pillows, blankets and stuffed toys can be vacuum-packed to cut volume by 50–70%. Measure the *packed* carton and round up to the next cm. — [Shipmozo volumetric guide](https://www.shipmozo.com/blog/volumetric-weight-calculator); [iCarry volumetric](https://www.icarry.in/pages/blog/volumetric-weight-shipping.html)

### Inferences
- **Zone D is the planning zone.** For a dropshipper whose supplier ships from one city (often Gujarat, Delhi NCR or Mumbai) to customers all over India, most orders fall in Zone C or D. A planning rate of ₹38–57 per first 500g before GST is safer than the ₹19–26 headline "starting" rates, which are Zone A/B on the cheapest courier.
- **Plan choice.** Paid Shiprocket plans (₹499/₹799) only pay off when the per-500g discount times monthly volume exceeds the fee. At ₹6/500g saving (₹26 vs ₹20), Pro breaks even at roughly 130 half-kg shipments/month. Start on Lite or another free tier.
- **RTO cost model.** Model RTO as forward freight charged a second time (100%, the conservative end of the 80–100% range), with COD fee probably reversed (Shiprocket) and GST on both legs. GST paid on freight is claimable as input credit only if the store is GST-registered.
- **Volumetric weight for curtains.** A 1.2 kg curtain pair in a 35×30×12 cm box bills at 2.52 kg, rounded up to 3 kg (6 × 500g slabs). Folded flat in a poly mailer (~35×30×5 cm = 1.05 kg volumetric), it bills at its 1.2 kg actual weight, rounded up to 1.5 kg (3 slabs). That roughly halves freight. This is an illustrative calculation, not a sourced quote. Pillows and cushions need vacuum packing; otherwise freight can exceed the product's margin.
- **Remittance cycle and cash flow.** COD cash is locked for days, which matters for ad-funded growth. NimbusPost's free T+3 compares favourably with paying 0.99% for Shiprocket's D+2. At 100 COD orders × ₹800, 0.99% costs about ₹792/month.

### Gaps
- No official, full zone-wise rate card (including additional-500g and 1/2/5 kg slab rates) was readable for any aggregator, because direct fetches were blocked. Additional-500g rates are not sourced. The worked examples below assume each extra 500g costs the same as the first, which likely overstates heavy-parcel cost.
- Shiprocket's standard (non-early) COD remittance cycle and its minimum wallet recharge were not found in any 2026 source. The same is true of the wallet minimums for NimbusPost, iThink and Shipway.
- Whether Shiprocket refunds the COD *fee* on RTO, and the exact RTO freight percentage per aggregator, are not confirmed.
- No verified 2026 COD fee figure was found specifically for NimbusPost, iThink or Shipway.

## 2. Shipping when using Indian dropshipping suppliers (Roposo Clout, Baapstore, DeoDap)

### Takeaway
Indian dropship suppliers ship from their own warehouses and charge the store owner either a per-product shipping fee or a shipping cost built into the product price. The **dropshipper, not the supplier, bears RTO and return costs**: Baapstore charges about ₹99 per RTO/return, and Roposo Clout pre-set weight-based RTO/RVP fees. Damage claims need fast proof, such as DeoDap's 48-hour unboxing-video rule. Roposo Clout reportedly stopped taking new orders on 1 April 2026, so plan without it.

### Cited Findings
- **Roposo Clout status:** several 2026 sources report it stopped taking new orders on 1 April 2026. Product listings, order placement and invoice generation are reportedly no longer active. One source words this less definitively ("reportedly shutting down"). — [Grunx: Roposo Clout shutdown 2026](https://grunx.in/blogs/blog/grunx-roposo-clout-shutdown-in-2026); [CIIM](https://www.ciim.in/dropshipping-in-india-is-roposo-closing-best-alternatives-you-must-know-2026-guide/); [Qikink](https://qikink.com/blog/roposo-clout-dropshipping/); [GridRay](https://gridray.com/blog/roposo-clout-closed-best-alternatives-2026/)
- **Roposo Clout RTO model (historical):** listings carried pre-set RTO and RVP (reverse pickup) charges based on product weight, applied automatically during order processing. The Shopify listing says "additional RTO and RVP charges may be applicable". — [Qikink](https://qikink.com/blog/roposo-clout-dropshipping/); [Shopify App Store: Roposo Clout](https://apps.shopify.com/roposo-clout)
- Roposo Clout seller complaints (anecdotal) describe RTO fees charged on orders that were actually delivered, with the burden of proof on the seller. — [AppNavigator 1-star review](https://appnavigator.io/app/roposo-clout/reviews/1669877); [Shopify reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/roposo-clout/reviews)
- **Baapstore:** pricing varies across its own pages. Shipping is a flat ₹79 or ₹89 per product (charged per product, not per order). The FAQ says "shipping starts @ ₹40". A third-party review cites a ₹39 fixed fee, and the Pro plan reportedly cuts shipping to ₹69. "Returns & RTOs cost ₹99", deducted from the wallet. Plans: Starter ₹4,999/year; Pro ₹24,999/year or ₹64,999 lifetime. Delivery takes 4–8 days. — [Baapstore pricing](https://www.baapstore.com/pricing); [Baapstore FAQ](https://baapstore.com/pages/faqs); [Baapstore dropship plans](https://shop.baapstore.com/pages/pricing); [DoDropshipping review](https://dodropshipping.com/best-dropshipping-suppliers-in-india/)
- **Baapstore branding:** "strict whitelabel dropshipping", with the courier label carrying only your brand/store name. Branded invoicing appears on the Starter plan; "branded package dropshipping" is listed on a higher tier. No custom prints. — [Baapstore pricing](https://www.baapstore.com/pricing); [Baapstore packages](https://products.baapstore.com/pricing)
- **DeoDap:** damaged or defective items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with a clear unboxing video (dropshipping terms and FAQ). The shipping subdomain's policy says 7 days, so the two conflict. The dropship partner pays return shipping, and shipping charges are non-refundable. — [DeoDap dropshipping T&C](https://dropshipping.deodap.com/terms-and-conditions.php); [DeoDap FAQs](https://dropshipping.deodap.com/faqs.php); [DeoDap shipping return policy](https://shipping.deodap.com/pages/return-and-refund-policy)
- DeoDap consumer reviews describe damage claims rejected for lacking an unboxing video. — [Trustpilot: DeoDap](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/deodap.in?page=2)
- Other Roposo alternatives named in 2026 guides include IndiaMART manufacturers, DeoDap, Qikink (print on demand), Snazzyway (fashion), GridRay and global apps (CJ, EPROLO, AutoDS). ### Inferences
- **Effective supplier-shipped RTO cost.** With Baapstore-type terms, a COD RTO costs the dropshipper the non-refundable forward shipping (₹40–89) plus the ₹99 RTO fee, about ₹140–190 per failed order. That is comparable to or higher than self-shipping via an aggregator. At a 25% COD RTO rate, it adds about ₹35–47 to the cost of every COD order *shipped*.
- **COD remittance through suppliers.** Supplier-collected COD typically reaches the dropshipper via the supplier's wallet or payout cycle, adding a second layer of cash-flow delay. Confirm each supplier's payout timing.
- **Fragile items.** The dropshipper absorbs customer complaints about damage but can only recover from suppliers with fast photo or video proof. For fragile items, budget a damage write-off.
- **Home-furnishing supplier pricing.** Suppliers charging *per product* rather than per order make multi-item baskets expensive. Volumetric weight on bulky items (cushions, curtains) is often baked into higher supplier shipping fees.

### Gaps
- DeoDap's per-order shipping fee and its explicit RTO charge/refund rule were not found on official pages.
- Baapstore's current (Oct 2026) authoritative figures are uncertain because its own pages conflict.
- No source documented COD remittance timing from these suppliers to the dropshipper.

## 3. COD share and RTO rates in Indian e-commerce (COD vs prepaid, by category, by tier)

### Takeaway
Plan for COD at roughly 50–65% of orders if COD is offered. Expect COD RTO around 24–26% on average (higher in tier-2/3 and in the festive season, up to ~35–40%) and prepaid RTO around 2–3%. Fashion and general merchandise run highest (25–40%). Many widely quoted figures come from vendors or have weak sourcing.

### Cited Findings
- **Shipway ShipNotes (FY25, published ~29 Jul 2025):**
  - COD RTO ~26%; prepaid RTO under 2%.
  - City spread among cities with ≥5,000 COD orders: Vadodara lowest at 18%, Patna highest at 35%.
  - Higher-RTO group: Patna, Srinagar, Jaipur, Varanasi, Ranchi, Jammu, Meerut, Guwahati, Visakhapatnam, Nashik.
  - Lower-RTO group: Vadodara, Thrissur, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Ahmedabad, Ernakulam, Nagpur, Chennai, New Delhi.
  - Delivery speed: orders attempted within 1–2 days had 22% RTO, within 3–5 days 27%, and after more than 5 days 35%.

  — [Mediabrief](https://mediabrief.com/shipnotes-reveals-26-rto-rate-on-cod-orders-across-india/); [Ordrio](https://ordrio.com/blog/courier-billing-volumetric-weight-cod-rto/); [Angadi Labs benchmarks](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- **Unicommerce India D2C Report 2026 (April 2026; 410M+ shipments, 6,000+ brands):**
  - RTO peaked at 39.2% in November 2025 (festive).
  - Brands applying operational fixes fell to 25.6% in January and 21.0% in March 2026.
  - COD orders "returned at 58%" in the festive quarter vs prepaid under 15%. This is a *return* rate, not strictly RTO.
  - Tier 2 and 3 cities drove 66% of incremental order volumes in FY26.

  — [Unicommerce India D2C Report 2026](https://unicommerce.com/india-d2c-report-2026-april/)
- **ProfitBox360 (2025):** COD is ~62.5% of Indian e-commerce orders but 53.1% of sales value. COD RTO is ~24.3% vs prepaid 2.8%. Tier-2 COD RTO is 29% and tier-3-and-below 37.5%. — [ProfitBox360](https://www.profitbox360.com/research/india/ecommerce/cod-prepaid-rto-rates-india)
- **Sourcing critique:**
  - The widely repeated "COD share" figure traces back to ET Prime Research (2024) via secondary write-ups.
  - Bain/Flipkart *How India Shops Online* contains no absolute COD-share or RTO figure.
  - Unicommerce publishes return rates but not COD share.
  - GoKwik's COD base rate is about 26% vs Unicommerce's 58% festive figure, which are different metrics.

  — [BotSense](https://botsense.io/blog/cod-verification-rto-numbers-india/)
- **Bain/Flipkart How India Shops Online 2026 (covering 2025):** tier-2+ cities contributed about 50% of incremental online orders in 2025, despite shopper penetration of 25–30% vs 45–50% in metro/tier-1. The share of COD users paying by UPI at the door rose 40% (2025 vs 2020), i.e. "COD is shifting to UPI-on-delivery". — [How India Shops Online 2026 PDF](https://storage.googleapis.com/flipkart-stories-media/How_India_Shops_Online_2026_060094273e/How_India_Shops_Online_2026_060094273e.pdf); [Bain HISO 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/how-india-shops-online-2025/)
- An earlier Bain-Flipkart edition said tier-2 and smaller cities contribute 3 of every 5 orders. — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/online-shoppers-in-tier-2-smaller-cities-contribute-to-3-out-of-every-5-orders-bain-flipkart-report)
- **Overall RTO estimates:** 25–35% (attributed to GoKwik, via ShipSagar) and 20–30% (Base). Fashion and general merchandise run 30–40% RTO in some categories. Fashion and apparel run 25–40%, per blogs without a clear primary source. — [ShipSagar](https://shipsagar.com/how-to-reduce-rto-rate-ecommerce-india/); [Base blog](https://base.com/en-IN/blog/rto-rates-in-india/); [FirstResort fashion RTO 2026](https://www.firstresort.in/blogs/research/fashion-ecommerce-returns-rto-india-2026)
- A generic calculator page puts COD RTO at 15–25% vs 2–5% for prepaid. Each RTO costs forward shipping, return shipping, the COD fee and wasted packaging. — [iCarry courier charges blog](https://www.icarry.in/pages/blog/courier-charges-india.html)

### Inferences
- **RTO planning assumptions** for a new, unknown-brand dropship store selling impulse products via Meta/Instagram ads to all-India audiences:
  - Base case: 25–30% COD RTO.
  - Festive season and tier-3 skew: 35–40%.
  - Prepaid: ~2–3%.

  New stores lack the trust signals that lower RTO, so they should land at or above industry averages.
- **COD share** will likely sit around 55–70% of orders for a new store if COD is offered without nudges. This is inferred from the ~62.5% average plus a new brand's lower trust.
- **Delivery speed.** Shipway's data links RTO to time-to-delivery (22% → 35%). Supplier dispatch speed is therefore an RTO lever, and a supplier taking 2–3 days to dispatch structurally raises RTO.

### Gaps
- No Redseer primary data on COD share or RTO was found in search results.
- No reliable home-furnishing/home-décor category RTO figure was found.
- The Bain/Flipkart tier-2 COD share percentage was not extractable from snippets.

## 4. Tactics to reduce RTO and fake orders, and claimed results

### Takeaway
The standard stack is:
1. Instant COD confirmation via WhatsApp (and/or IVR).
2. Prepaid discounts.
3. Partial COD (a ₹50–200 advance) or a COD fee for risky segments.
4. Pincode/customer risk blocking.
5. Address verification.
6. Faster delivery.

Vendor-reported results are typically a 15–18% relative reduction in RTO, with headline claims up to 40–45%. None are independently verified, and most bundle several tactics together.

### Cited Findings
- **GoKwik case studies (vendor-reported):**
  - FanCode: RTO down 15.5% in one month via COD confirmation pop-ups and capping COD at ₹1,500.
  - Be Bodywise: RTO down 15%, using a COD confirmation prompt offering a digital-payment alternative; AWB end-state accuracy rose from 80% to 98%.

- **Faster delivery:** RTO rises from 22% (1–2 day attempt) to 35% (>5 days). — [Mediabrief](https://mediabrief.com/shipnotes-reveals-26-rto-rate-on-cod-orders-across-india/)
- **Operational fixes at scale:** Unicommerce brands applying operational fixes cut RTO from a 39.2% peak to 21.0% over about four months. — [Unicommerce D2C Report 2026](https://unicommerce.com/india-d2c-report-2026-april/)

### Inferences
- **Low-budget stack for a new Shopify dropship store:**
  - A WhatsApp COD-confirmation app at ≈₹0.1–7 per order (cheap relative to a ₹100–190 RTO loss).
  - A ₹50–100 prepaid discount.
  - Partial COD (e.g. ₹99 advance) on orders above ~₹1,000 or in high-RTO pincodes.
  - Cancel unconfirmed COD orders before dispatch.

  A realistic target is to move from about 28% to about 20–23% COD RTO. That is a 15–25% relative reduction, consistent with GoKwik's audited-style case studies, not the 45% marketing claims.
- **Break-even for confirmation.** If confirmation costs ₹3/order and avoids one RTO (≈₹130–190) per 100 COD orders, it pays for itself many times over. The main hidden cost is cancelled genuine orders.
- **Shopify UPI-on-delivery.** The Bain data on rising UPI-on-delivery suggests customers want "pay at door" assurance more than cash itself. Prepaid nudges framed around trust (easy refunds, tracking) may convert better than blunt COD fees.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) controlled study isolating the effect of WhatsApp confirmation, partial COD or COD fees on Indian RTO was found.
- IVR confirmation pricing and results were not found.
- Address-verification tool accuracy figures were not found.

## 5. Packaging costs per order and branded packaging/invoices via suppliers

Plain poly mailers cost about ₹1–6 each. Small-run custom-printed mailers cost about ₹9 each at 100 units. 3-ply corrugated boxes cost about ₹5–25 (plus ₹3–15 for printing), and 5-ply boxes ₹12–80. When dropshipping, the supplier packs. Some suppliers (e.g. Baapstore) offer white-label courier labels and branded invoices, with fully branded packaging only on higher tiers.

## 6. Worked examples: one 500g and one 1.5 kg parcel, prepaid vs COD vs COD-RTO

### Takeaway
On mid-market Zone D rates, a COD order costs roughly ₹40–50 more to deliver than prepaid. A COD RTO costs ₹95–190 (500g) or ₹290–470 (1.5 kg) with zero revenue. Spread across all COD orders at a 26% RTO rate, the shipping cost per *successfully delivered* COD order is about 2.2–2.4× the prepaid equivalent for a 500g parcel and about 1.8× for a 1.5 kg parcel.

### Cited Findings (inputs used)
| Input | Value used | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Zone D first-500g freight, Low scenario | ₹38 | [shipagent.in](https://www.shipagent.in/rate-calculator) |
| Zone D first-500g freight, High scenario | ₹57 | Delhivery 3rd-party card via [ClickPost](https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/delhivery-courier-charges) / [edesy](https://edesy.in/tools/shipping-rate-calculator) |
| COD fee, Low | ₹35 or 1.8%, whichever is higher | [shipagent.in](https://www.shipagent.in/rate-calculator) |

| GST on freight and COD fee | 18% | [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/shiprocket/pricing) |

Assumptions (not sourced):
- Order values: 500g item at ₹699; 1.5 kg item at ₹1,499.
- 1.5 kg is billed as 3 × 500g slabs at the first-slab rate. Real additional-slab or 1–2 kg slab rates are often lower, so this overstates.
- No fuel surcharge (aggregators usually bundle it). Delhivery direct adds about 10%.
- COD fee is reversed on RTO, with the not-reversed case shown in brackets.
- Payment-gateway fees on prepaid orders are excluded.

**Per-shipment cost, Zone D, incl. 18% GST and packaging (₹)**

| Scenario | 500g Low | 500g High | 1.5 kg Low | 1.5 kg High |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prepaid, delivered | 49.84 | 72.26 | 154.52 | 221.78 |
| COD, delivered | 91.14 | 119.46 | 195.82 | 268.98 |
| COD, RTO (forward + return freight + lost packaging; COD fee reversed) | 94.68 [135.98 if COD fee kept] | 139.52 [186.72] | 289.04 [330.34] | 423.56 [470.76] |

Arithmetic, 500g High:
- Prepaid: 57 × 1.18 + 5 = 72.26.
- COD delivered: (57 + 40) × 1.18 + 5 = 119.46.
- RTO: (57 + 57) × 1.18 + 5 = 139.52.

Arithmetic, 1.5 kg High:
- Prepaid: 171 × 1.18 + 20 = 221.78.
- COD delivered: (171 + 40) × 1.18 + 20 = 268.98.
- RTO: 342 × 1.18 + 20 = 423.56.

**Blended cost per order shipped and per order delivered (26% COD RTO, 2% prepaid RTO)**

| | 500g Low | 500g High | 1.5 kg Low | 1.5 kg High |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COD: expected shipping cost per order shipped | 92.06 | 124.68 | 220.06 | 309.17 |
| COD: shipping cost per *delivered* order (÷0.74) | 124.41 | 168.48 | 297.37 | 417.80 |
| Prepaid: shipping cost per delivered order (÷0.98) | 51.77 | 75.11 | 160.42 | 230.42 |

### Inferences
- **What COD and RTO add.** On a ₹699 product, COD plus RTO adds about ₹73–93 of shipping cost per delivered order versus prepaid (≈10–13% of the selling price). This comes on top of wasted ad spend on the returned order, which is usually the bigger loss and is out of scope here.
- **Ad-spend sensitivity.** If customer acquisition cost is ₹200 per order placed, every RTO also wastes that ₹200.
- **Holding RTO stock.** RTO'd stock may return damaged or be unsellable. With supplier dropshipping, the product may not come back to the store owner at all, and only the fee (e.g. Baapstore ₹99) is charged.
- **Volumetric weight for home furnishing.** A boxed 3 kg pillow billing at 14.4 kg would cost about ₹1,650 pre-GST at a linear ₹57/500g in Zone D (illustrative; heavy-slab rates are lower). Vacuum-packed to 3.6 kg, billed at 4 kg, it would cost about ₹456. An RTO on the un-compressed version would cost about ₹3,300+ in freight. For GD Home Fabric products, compressing or flat-folding is essential before offering COD nationwide.
- **When COD stops paying.** COD is economically viable for low-weight, mid-ticket items (₹500–1,500) with confirmation and partial COD. For heavy or bulky items, prepaid-only or partial-COD-only is advisable because RTO cost scales with weight.

### Gaps
- The examples rely on third-party benchmark rates, because official Shiprocket/NimbusPost Zone D and additional-500g rates could not be fetched. Re-run with live calculator quotes (the pincode-to-pincode rate calculator in any aggregator dashboard) before setting prices.
- Payment-gateway fees for prepaid orders (needed for a true prepaid-vs-COD comparison) were not researched here.
- The cost of handling a customer-initiated return (reverse pickup, RVP) was not quantified. Roposo Clout and Baapstore charge RVP and returns fees, but no current aggregator RVP rate was found.
